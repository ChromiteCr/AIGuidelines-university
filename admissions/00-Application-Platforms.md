# Application Platforms & UC System: Official AI Guidance — Source Text

- Accessed: 2026-09-24 (Asia/Shanghai; exact acquisition timestamps in `research/platforms.json`)
- Format note: Selected verbatim English excerpts with separate Chinese analysis; not a complete reproduction or legal interpretation.
- Scope note: Common App is an application platform; UC sources concern its shared undergraduate application. These are **not additional universities** and must not be counted as independently issued rules at every member/campus.
- Evidence note: All quoted text has been fetched from official public sources and matched against saved text. Common App's static HTML only exposed headings; its official public Gatsby page-data JSON contained the actual policy paragraphs. No login or access-control bypass was used.

## 关键发现

1. **Common App 禁止的是冒认实质性 AI 产出为本人原创。** 必须保留 `intentionally misrepresenting`、`one’s own original work`、`substantive content or output` 的条件，不能缩写成“使用任何 AI 就是申请欺诈”。该文件没有逐项授权语法检查、构思、翻译或改写；这些边界仍须查目标院校。[CA1]
2. **平台后果与大学录取决定不同。** Common App 可暂停、终止账号并向 My Colleges List 通报；大学如何处置录取是另一个层级，不能把平台处分直接等同于必然退学。[CA1]
3. **Common App 明文提供申诉机会。** 针对平台自身的违规认定，注册用户可以在书面通知后 10 个工作日内提交陈述与证据。这并不保证每所大学都有同样的录取申诉程序，但足以说明不能笼统写“申请审查没有任何陈述或申诉机会”。[CA1-Q2]
4. **UC 官方材料区分辅助与代写。** 官网招生人员访谈允许谨慎利用 AI 辅助结构、可读性；2025 年招生顾问培训材料承认早期阶段的辅助作用，但要求最后的 PIQ 在声音、实质和反思上属于申请人本人。不能据此理解为可直接提交 AI 改写后的任何内容。[UC1, UC3]
5. **UC 也有明确标注 2026–27 申请季的指导。** 现场下载的 first-year 申请指南共 84 页，第 67 页将 AI 定位为早期构思/整理思路的工具，而非本人作品与声音的替代。搜索摘要仍可能显示旧版，不应照抄摘要。[UC5]
6. **UC 有直接点名生成式 AI 的申请诚信声明。** 本轮找到并下载公开官方 `Statement of Application Integrity`：允许就内容、编辑获得建议，包括用生成式 AI 辅助可读性，但内容和最终文字必须属于本人；UC 可要求核实内容或写作确系本人所作。该正式系统文件比招生访谈更适合作为规则依据。[UC4]

### [UC4] Statement of Application Integrity

- Publishing unit: University of California undergraduate admissions application
- Official source: https://apply.universityofcalifornia.edu/docs/StatementOfIntegrity.pdf
- Page date: Last updated August 2023
- Scope: UC undergraduate application; shared system rule; public PDF page 1
- Authority: system-application-policy
- Saved text: `sources/platform-uc-S4.txt`

**[UC4-Q1]**

> The personal insight responses should reflect the student’s own ideas and experiences, be independently written by the student and reflect the student’s personal writing style. Students may receive advice on content and editing, including the use of generative artificial intelligence software to assist with readability, but content and final written text must be their own.

**[UC4-Q2]**

> UC conducts regular screenings to verify the integrity of the responses, may request authentication of the content or writing as the student’s, and will take action when it is determined that the integrity of the response is compromised.

**[UC4-Q3]**

> Students found to have falsified application information will be subject to penalties up to and including denial of admission, withdrawal of an admission offer, registration cancellation, expulsion or revocation of a University of California degree.

### [CA1] Common Application Fraud Policy

- Scope: Common App registered users; platform policy, not an individual university policy

- Saved text: `sources/platform-commonapp-S1.txt`

**[CA1-Q1]**

> Submitting plagiarized essays or other written or oral material, or intentionally misrepresenting as one’s own original work: (1) another person’s thoughts, language, ideas, expressions, or experiences or (2) the substantive content or output of an artificial intelligence platform, technology, or algorithm;

**[CA1-Q2]**

> A Registered User who receives notice that Common App has determined a violation of our Terms of Use occurred shall be provided an opportunity to appeal Common App’s decision within ten (10) business days from the date of Common App’s written notice by submitting a written statement and any relevant supporting documentation refuting the Fraud determination or otherwise explaining the circumstances around the misconduct.

**[CA1-Q3]**

> Common App in its sole discretion may take action as a result of a Fraud determination which may include permanent suspension, temporary suspension, or termination of their Common App user account, and disclosure of the Fraud to the Registered User’s “My Colleges List."

- UC 的三所样本学校可共同受到系统 PIQ 要求约束，但在跨校统计中必须标记 **共同来源**，不可作为三份相互独立的政策证据。
- 原文提及 plagiarism detection 不等于公布 AI 检测器名称、准确率或“仅凭分数作决定”的程序，不能自行补推。
- 本文件不替代学校或申请平台当季页面；特殊项目、作品集、视频面试和推荐信可能另有要求。
