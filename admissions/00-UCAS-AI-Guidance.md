# UCAS: Application AI Guidance — Shared Platform Supplement

- Research snapshot: **2026-09-26** (Asia/Shanghai). Exact acquisition timestamps appear below and in the [evidence JSON](../research/college-fair-2026-09-26/sol-1041/ucas-data.json).
- Scope: UCAS 本科申请个人陈述及相关真实性、相似度和申诉程序。**平台材料，不计为额外学校，不计作英国四校各自独立发布的政策。**
- Format: selected original-language quotations + Chinese research interpretation; excerpts are not the full policy. Publication/effective dates were not explicitly identified; access dates are not publication dates.

## 核心结论

1. **AI 辅助不等于 AI 代写。** UCAS 指导举例支持主题构思、结构建议、可读性检查，但最终个人陈述应反映申请者自己的经历与表达；页面明确把直接复制 AI 文字作为最终文本列入 Don’t。[UCAS-S1]
2. **保留页面内部的细节，不能简化成“所有起草都禁用”。** 同一指南还介绍 Copilot 可由本人笔记生成段落初稿。它是工具示例，不是许可将该段落直接作为最终文书提交；需结合 Don’t 项及本科申请声明阅读。是否符合某校更窄的使用边界，还须单独核查。[UCAS-S1][UCAS-S6]
3. **声明比一般工具介绍更直接关联提交。** 本科申请声明要求个人陈述由本人完成；若材料可能来自他人或 AI，UCAS 可通知相关机构与申请院校，由院校决定适当行动。不是每次 AI 辅助都必然取消申请。[UCAS-S6]
4. **相似度检查不等于 AI 检测。** 官方 PDF 描述与历年文书及网页样例库的文本比对、人员复核和通知流程；大于 30% 的相似度触发所述人工复核，并不是“30% 以下允许抄袭”或“AI 含量阈值”。[UCAS-S5]
5. **两种程序不能混淆。** UCAS 取消申请有申诉程序，核验 PDF 给出取消后 28 天提交申诉及书面答复安排；而相似度报告 PDF 则称不预期对报告本身存在申诉理由。平台取消的申诉权不能自动外推为对大学录取决定的申诉权。[UCAS-S4][UCAS-S5][UCAS-S6]
6. **英国四校仍分别核查。** UCAS 明确建议查各校的 AI 指导；LSE、Oxford、Cambridge、Imperial 的校方或项目规则见对应学校档案。本补充不是四校统一许可清单。[UCAS-S1]

### [UCAS-S1] A guide to using AI and ChatGPT with your personal statement
- Official source: https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/a-guide-to-using-ai-and-chatgpt-with-your-personal-statement
- Final URL: https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/a-guide-to-using-ai-and-chatgpt-with-your-personal-statement
- Publishing unit: UCAS
- Authority: official-platform-advice
- Scope: UCAS personal statement applicants; general advice, subject to institution-specific rules
- Acquired: 2026-09-26T02:18:51.975021+00:00
- Snapshot: [sources/00-UCAS-S1.txt](sources/00-UCAS-S1.txt)
- Context: Q4 is the object of the page’s Don’t heading. The full source contains brainstorming and structure suggestions, and an explicitly described Copilot paragraph-drafting example; none removes the final authorship/declaration requirement.

**UCAS-S1-Q1 — limited support, not outsourced authorship**
> You could use ChatGPT as a tool to help inspire, clarify and articulate your own ideas, rather than asking it to write your entire personal statement for you.

**UCAS-S1-Q2 — readability**
> ChatGPT can be used to check your personal statement draft for readability. It may suggest ways to rephrase sentences to make them more concise, while maintaining their meaning.

**UCAS-S1-Q3 — Copilot example must be read with final-text prohibition**
> It can create a first draft of a paragraph from a few bullet points from your notes, or summarise your first draft if you're over the character count.

**UCAS-S1-Q4 — item under Don’t heading, not permission**
> Copy and paste from AI tools and use as final text.

**UCAS-S1-Q5 — institution-specific guidance**
> We'd advise researching what individual universities and colleges recommend you include in your personal statement, and whether they have specific guidance around using AI tools like ChatGPT.

### [UCAS-S6] UCAS Undergraduate declaration
- Official source: https://www.ucas.com/node/866
- Final URL: https://www.ucas.com/about-us/policies/ucas-data-privacy-portal/ucas-undergraduate-declaration
- Publishing unit: UCAS
- Authority: application-declaration
- Scope: Applicants submitting a UCAS Undergraduate application
- Acquired: 2026-09-26T02:44:26.130674+00:00
- Snapshot: [sources/00-UCAS-S6.txt](sources/00-UCAS-S6.txt)
- Context: Read publicly without entering the application system or accepting the declaration.

**UCAS-S6-Q1 — authorship**
> Your personal statement must be completed by you and we do undertake checks to verify that it is your own work.

**UCAS-S6-Q2 — notification; universities decide action**
> If your personal statement includes material that we reasonably consider may have been copied from or provided by another source, including but not limited to friends, writing service providers or artificial intelligence software, we may notify your current school, college or other UCAS registered centre (provided that, where legally required, we have your permission to share such information) and the universities and colleges to which you have applied, whose decision it will be to take what action they consider appropriate.

**UCAS-S6-Q3 — platform cancellation appeal**
> If we cancel your application you have the right to appeal.

**UCAS-S6-Q4 — not university-decision appeal**
> The appeal process relates only to applicants whose application has been cancelled by us.
