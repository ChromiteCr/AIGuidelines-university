## 关键发现（中文）

- Yale 本科招生办明确将其 AI 声明标为本校政策：提交 AI 实质内容或输出构成申请欺诈；由文本生成软件写成的个人陈述或其他书面回答可能导致撤销录取或开除。[S1]
- 同一政策明确允许 AI 检查语法或拼写，以及在写作开始阶段寻求一般建议或选题建议。因此不能把 Yale 写成“全面禁 AI”或“只能拼写检查”。[S1]
- 补充材料说明允许一年级与转学申请者提交艺术或学术研究材料；STEM 表要求说明合作研究中本人的具体贡献。公开说明页没有另外列出 AI 使用或披露细则，不应凭空补成科研 AI 禁令。[S2]

**[S1-Q1]**

> As detailed in the above statements, it is Yale’s policy that submitting “the substantive content or output of an artificial intelligence platform, technology, or algorithm” constitutes application fraud. Submitting personal statements or other written application responses composed by text-generating software may result in admission revocation or expulsion.

**[S1-Q2]**

> Using an AI platform to review one’s grammar or spelling, or to seek general advice or topic suggestions at the start of the writing process does not constitute application fraud. Some applicants may find AI tools useful in these ways; others may not.

**[S2-Q1]**

> First-year and transfer applicants may supplement their application with an arts portfolio or academic research.

**[S2-Q2]**

> Complete the STEM Research Supplement form to describe your research and your specific contributions if the work was done in collaboration with others. Use the form to attach a research paper, abstract, or poster. Full research papers are generally the most helpful. You may also use the form to invite a research mentor to submit a letter of recommendation electronically to the admissions office.

## Permitted / prohibited / not stated（按用途）

- **明确允许：**语法或拼写检查；写作开始时的一般建议、选题建议。[S1]
- **禁止 / 欺诈边界：**AI 实质性内容或输出用于申请；文本生成软件代写个人陈述及其他书面回答。不是一切接触 AI 都禁止。[S1]
- **未单独说明：**翻译、AI 提纲、逐句改写/润色的界线、研究中使用 AI 的具体规则、AI 披露格式、检测方法。不得把一般建议扩展为允许代写。
- **研究补充材料：**公开说明要求交代本人对合作研究的具体贡献；这不是“AI 使用无需披露”的证明。[S2]
- **可能后果：**撤销录取或开除，原文为 may result。[S1]
