## 关键发现（中文）

- 本科 FAQ 明确允许 AI 调研学校、头脑风暴文书题目、检查已经写完文书的语法和拼写。[S1]
- AI 列提纲、起草或代写文书、将另一种语言写成的文书翻译过来、生成必需作品集图像，均被 FAQ 列为不合伦理；正式 Receiving Help 政策进一步以申请回答及作品集作品表述禁止范围。[S1][S2]
- 选题建议不等于由别人替你决定题目；Receiving Help 也不接受他人写/重写回答、替选题目或套用网上文书模板。提交申请即确认没有采用不可接受的帮助。[S2]
- 一般撤录政策允许因欺诈、虚假陈述、重大遗漏等撤销录取，甚至在到校后；这是一般规则，不是“凡用 AI 就自动撤录”。[S3]

### 1. [S1] Can prospective students use generative AI to help with their application to Cornell?

- Official source: https://faq.enrollment.cornell.edu/kb/article/428-can-prospective-students-use-generative-ai-to-help-with-their-application-to-cornell/
- Issuing unit: Cornell Undergraduate Admissions
- Page date: not stated
- Scope: undergraduate
- Authority kind: policy (official admissions FAQ)
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24T23:06:36+08:00; full-text (ordinary public HTTP request)
- Local readable text: [sources/05-Cornell-S1.txt](sources/05-Cornell-S1.txt)

**[S1-Q1]**

> If you choose to use generative AI in your application process, we expect you to do so ethically. Ethical uses of generative AI include researching colleges, brainstorming essay topics, and reviewing the grammar and spelling of your completed essays. Unethical uses include (but are not limited to) using generative AI to outline, draft, or write your essays; translating an essay written in another language; and creating images for a required portfolio.

### 2. [S2] Receiving Help with Your Application

- Official source: https://admissions.cornell.edu/policies/receiving-help-with-your-application
- Issuing unit: Cornell University Office of Undergraduate Admissions
- Page date: not stated
- Scope: undergraduate
- Authority kind: policy
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24T23:06:49+08:00; full-text (ordinary public HTTP request)
- Local readable text: [sources/05-Cornell-S2.txt](sources/05-Cornell-S2.txt)

**[S2-Q1]**

> Help that is ethical and acceptable to utilize in your application process includes: Using AI tools to research specific colleges or help you build your college list. Working with your college counselor or a community-based organization staff member to brainstorm essay topics. Asking a friend or family member to read your essays and give feedback or check for grammatical errors.

**[S2-Q2]**

> However, there are unethical – and therefore unacceptable – forms of application assistance as well. These include: Having AI tools outline, draft, or write your application responses, translate your responses from another language, or create works for a required portfolio. Having any person write or rewrite your essays/responses. Having someone else choose your essay topics. Using a template you found online to write your essay.

**[S2-Q3]**

> When you hit “submit” on your Common Application, you’re agreeing that you have not used unacceptable, unethical support resources to complete your application.

**中文说明：**正式本科政策页。允许清单里只有查学校明确提到 AI，构思和校对例子以真人为主体；AI 构思与语法检查的明确许可来自 [S1]，不能只凭 [S2] 自行换成 AI。禁止清单则直接点名 AI 列提纲、写作、翻译和作品集。

### 3. [S3] Admission Revocation Policy

- Official source: https://admissions.cornell.edu/policies/admission-revocation-policy
- Issuing unit: Cornell University Office of Undergraduate Admissions
- Page date: not stated
- Scope: undergraduate; general admission-revocation wording
- Authority kind: policy
- Retrieved: 2026-09-24T23:06:50+08:00; full-text (ordinary public HTTP request)

**[S3-Q1]**

> Cornell University reserves the right to revoke an offer of admission under certain circumstances, and at any time, including after a student is in attendance at the university. Authority to revoke admission rests with the admitting academic unit. Admission may be revoked for fraud, misrepresentation, material omission of fact, failure to meet a condition of admission (including but not limited to satisfactorily completing any schoolwork in progress at the time an offer of admission is extended and upholding high standards of character in all activities within and outside of the classroom), or any other pre-attendance misconduct.

## Permitted / prohibited / not stated（按用途）

- **明确允许：**AI 调研学校/建立学校清单、文书题目头脑风暴、检查已完成文书的语法和拼写。[S1][S2]
- **明确不接受：**AI 生成提纲、起草或写申请回答；AI 翻译另一语言的回答；AI 生成必需作品集作品（FAQ 特别举图像）。[S1][S2]
- **仍由本人负责：**题目最终选择、经历、观点与实际措辞；校对许可不是 AI 实质重写许可。[S2]
- **未单独说明：**具体 AI 披露模板、AI 检测流程、可接受语法改动的字数/比例阈值、研究补充材料内的 AI 细则。不能由这些留白推定可使用。
- **后果：**一般欺诈/虚假陈述撤录政策适用；没有在已引 AI 清单中宣布所有违规均自动撤录。[S3]
