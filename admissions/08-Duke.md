**[S4-Q1] permitted editing versus generated content**

> These tools may be used to support editing and clarity, but your ideas, experiences, and responses should be your own. AI should help refine your content, not generate it.

**[S4-Q2] review and potential consequences**

> Essays may also be reviewed using AI-detection tools. Submissions that rely heavily on AI-generated content, misrepresent the applicant, or are written by another person may negatively affect an applicant's evaluation and may constitute a violation of the Fuqua Honor Code.

**[S4-Q3] attribution, plagiarism and denial**

> Using language or content from external sources, including websites, publications, other applicants' materials, consultants, or AI-generated content, without proper attribution constitutes plagiarism. Plagiarism is a violation of the Fuqua Honor Code and may result in denial of admission.

> 以下为研究者解读，不是校方原文；不把一般真实性期待写成未发布的 AI 规则。

- **本科：允许／禁止：** 均未查到明确 AI 分类；不要借用下列 MBA 规则填补本科空白。
