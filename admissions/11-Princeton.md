> Newer on the scene are tools that allow for the generation of ideas and actual composition of papers and essays. While it might be tempting to rely on these types of aides, here’s my two cents about why you should shy away from them when preparing your college essays.

## Limits and verification gaps

- Both sources are official advice by the Dean, not student opinion, but neither is a detailed formal AI-use code or list of automatic admission penalties.
- The 2026 article mentions spell/grammar tools as longstanding technologies; mere mention is not an express permission for all AI editing. The latest advice particularly cautions against generated ideas and composition.
- Neither page separately settles translation, research supplements, short answers, a disclosure procedure, or an AI-detector regime.
- The 2025 statement refers to an application attestation; the full attestation form was not accessed and should not be reconstructed from this short description.
- No silence is treated as permission; Common App platform rules and enrolled-student coursework policies are separate.
