## 中文结论 / Findings

- Brown 本科招生办明确：申请人在任何情况下都不得将 AI 用于申请内容；可以像使用其他基础校对工具一样，用 AI 检查拼写和语法；所有文书、短答及其他提交材料须为申请人本人的作品。[S1-Q1]
- Brown 认可 Common App 对申请欺诈的定义，其中包括将 AI 平台、技术或算法的实质性内容或输出冒认为本人原创。[S1-Q2]
- 页面引用的申请声明写明：所证明的信息如不实，申请人可能受到撤销录取、开除，或撤销学分、成绩与学位等处分；招生办每年对部分已决定入读的录取学生随机核验若干资料。[S1-Q3]
- 研究生 FAQ 的 AI 条款与本科措辞相同，但属于不同申请，各自适用。[S2]

**[S1-Q1] Undergraduate AI rule and proofreading exception**

> Brown affirms this definition and reiterates our own independent policy that the use of artificial intelligence by an applicant is not permitted under any circumstances in conjunction with application content. While an applicant may use artificial intelligence to assist with spelling and grammar review, in the same way as any other platform that supports basic proofreading, the content of all essays, short-answer questions and any other material submitted by an applicant must be the work of that individual.

**[S1-Q2] Common App definition affirmed by Brown**

> As per the Common App's policy linked above, their definition of application fraud includes: "submitting plagiarized essays or other written or oral material, or intentionally misrepresenting as one’s own original work: (1) another person’s thoughts, language, ideas, expressions, or experiences or (2) the substantive content or output of an artificial intelligence platform, technology, or algorithm."

**[S1-Q3] Certification statement and possible consequences**

> I certify that all information submitted in the admission process — including this application and any other supporting materials — is my own work, factually true, and honestly presented, and that these documents will become the property of the institution to which I am applying and will not be returned to me. I understand that I may be subject to a range of possible disciplinary actions, including admission revocation, expulsion, or revocation of course credit, grades, and degree should the information I have certified be false.

**中文说明：**这是 Brown 本科招生办的明确 AI 表态：先认可 Common App 的欺诈定义，再重申本校“独立政策”——申请内容一律不得使用 AI，唯一写明的例外是拼写和语法检查。S1-Q3 是页面引用的 Common App 申请声明；may be subject to 表示可能后果，不是自动适用。

## 使用边界（中文解读，非原文）

- **允许／可用：** 用 AI 检查拼写和语法，与其他基础校对工具同等对待。[S1-Q1]；研究生 S2 措辞相同。

- **可能后果：** 撤销录取、开除，或撤销学分、成绩与学位；原文为 may be subject to，不是自动适用。[S1-Q3]
