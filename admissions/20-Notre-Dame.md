**[S1-Q1] allowed assistance and own-work requirement**

> While an applicant may use artificial intelligence to assist with spelling and grammar review, or brainstorming support before writing, the content of all essays, short answer questions, activities, and any other material submitted must be an applicant’s own work.

**[S1-Q2] AI-generated/drafted content and possible consequences**

> Submitting content generated, drafted, or substantially written by artificial intelligence tools or other text-generating software may result in the revocation of your offer of admission or potential expulsion.

## 中文解释（非原文）

这是本科招生办公室的AI Policy，而非一般真实性倡议。不能把“允许头脑风暴”扩大成“AI先代写、本人再修改”。may result是可能后果，不是每次违规必然处分。该校页面虽明确采用Common App Fraud Policy，本条用法与后果依据其自身AI段落，不另移植平台规则。

## 允许 / 禁止 / 未说明

- **允许或认可的辅助**：spelling and grammar review；brainstorming support before writing。提交内容仍须由本人创作。[S1-Q1]
- **禁止或反对的使用**：提交AI或其他文本生成软件生成、起草或实质写成的内容，政策列明可能撤录/开除。[S1-Q2]
- **未明确说明**：翻译、深度改写界线、披露格式、AI比例阈值、工具名单和检测流程未单列。
