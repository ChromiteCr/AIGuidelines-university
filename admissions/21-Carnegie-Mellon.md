**[S1-Q1] supplementary assistance only**

> AI should never replace your unique voice, experiences and personal expression and, if used at all, should only serve as a supplementary tool to enhance your writing (i.e., grammar and spelling checks, suggested structural or vocabulary improvements).

**[S1-Q2] plagiarism risk; caution**

**[S2-Q1] graduate essay self-authorship**

> Your essay should contain only true information and your own, original content. You should not use artificial intelligence (AI), such as ChatGPT, to write any portion of your essay.

**[S2-Q2] graduate document verification; methods not specified**

> The Heinz College Office of Admissions utilizes several methods to identify plagiarized, AI-generated, and falsified content in uploaded documents.

**[S2-Q3] graduate possible denial**

> Submission of false, plagiarized or AI-generated material as part of the application for admission may result in automatic denial of admission to any degree program.
