**[S1-Q1] own ideas and experiences**

> Your submission should always reflect your own ideas, creativity, and experiences.

**[S1-Q2] editing, not creation**

> AI tools could polish or refine, but they should not replace or generate your personal input or creativity. If you choose to use AI for editing, treat it as a suggestive tool to enhance your writing, not to create it.

**[S1-Q3] activities and personal statement assistance**

> For example, AI might help you clarify your thoughts in the Activities section of the Common Application or organize ideas in a personal statement.

## 允许 / 禁止 / 未说明

- **允许或认可的辅助**：作为suggestive tool提供编辑/润色建议；澄清活动栏想法、组织个人陈述思路；本人思想、声音、经历不被替代。[S1-Q1][S1-Q2][S1-Q3]
- **禁止或反对的使用**：should not replace or generate个人内容或创意；AI用于enhance，而非create申请者的写作。[S1-Q2]
- **未明确说明**：AI翻译、从零生成草稿、专门披露、阈值、检测和申请期专项后果未明确给出。没有另写头脑风暴许可；仅凭组织思路不能推断无限制创意生成获准。
