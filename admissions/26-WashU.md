**[S1-Q1] prohibited brainstorming, authorship, portfolio images, fraud**

> Never Unethical uses include (but are not limited to) using AI to brainstorm topics, draft, or write your essays; generating images for your Sam Fox School of Design & Visual Arts portfolio; or producing fraudulent application materials such as a letter of recommendation, a transcript of high school courses, or a certificate for an award.

**[S1-Q2] college research allowed**

> Always Researching colleges and developing your list of colleges are ethical uses of AI.

**[S1-Q3] conditional proofreading and application research**

> Sometimes Reviewing the grammar and spelling of your completed essays can be a helpful use of AI, but you may ask a teacher or counselor to review as well. You can use AI to find answers to questions you have about the application process; beware that AI can be wrong or out of date!

### 可用 / Permitted or positively advised

研究大学和制定选校名单；有条件/审慎地检查已完成文书的语法拼写、查问申请流程（分别标为Always和Sometimes）。[S1]
