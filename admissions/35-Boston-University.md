**[S1-Q1] AI supports original ideas but not whole essay**

> If you opt to use these tools at any point while writing your essays, they should only be used to support your original ideas rather than to write your essays in their entirety.

**[S1-Q2] Credit others**

> When representing the words or ideas of another in their original work, students should properly credit the source.
