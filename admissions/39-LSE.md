# London School of Economics and Political Science: Undergraduate Application & AI Guidance

- Evidence status: `explicit-undergraduate-ai`
- Research acquisition: source-specific ISO dates below (not publication dates).
- Scope: 本科招生与个人陈述；研究生和在校生作业规则单独区分。

## 中文结论 / Findings

- [S1-Q1][S1-Q5] LSE 不鼓励生成式 AI 创作个人陈述，除质量提醒外，还明确保留对与以前提交文书显著相似或用AI创作的个人陈述拒绝申请的权利；应与同页有限辅助建议一起阅读，不是「任何 AI 使用一律禁止」。
- [S1-Q2][S1-Q3][S1-Q4] 构思、推荐阅读并与教师核对、校对原创文字是所举建设性用途。
- [S1] 2026 年 9 月入学起 UCAS 个人陈述分三问、总计 4,000 字符；仅接受通过 UCAS 提交的陈述。

**[S1-Q1] 不鼓励 AI 代写**

> We do not encourage applicants to use generative Artificial Intelligence tools to create their personal statement as they do not produce high-quality personal statements and may lead to rejection.

**[S1-Q2] 初步构思**

> generate initial ideas.

**[S1-Q3] 荐读需核对**

> suggest reading materials or references for you to explore (to be cross-checked with teachers for relevance).

**[S1-Q4] 校对本人文字**

> proofread original writing.

**[S1-Q5] 明确保留拒绝申请的权利**

> We reserve the right to reject your application where it has been found that a statement has significant similarities to a previous submission or has been created with the use of Artificial Intelligence (using tools such as ChatGPT).

## Limitations

- LSE 招生页面未提供每一种 AI 用途的披露模板或检测规则；不得把学生评估许可转为招生许可。

## High-school adaptation (researcher recommendation; not university policy)

- 每项课程、项目或评估先查教师书面规则；文书由申请人真实反思并亲自核对事实。
- 明确区分可用于构思或校对、可提交的内容与不可在面试／评估使用的工具；勿将大学在校作业规则照搬入高中或本科申请。
