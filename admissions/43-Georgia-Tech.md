**[S1-Q1] Helpful brainstorming, final authorship**

> AI tools can be powerful and valuable in the application process when used thoughtfully. We believe there is a place for them in helping you generate ideas, but your ultimate submission should be your own.

**[S1-Q2] No copying**

> As with all other sources, you should not copy and paste content you did not create directly into your application.

**[S1-Q3] Suggested process**

> Use it to brainstorm, edit, and refine your ideas.
