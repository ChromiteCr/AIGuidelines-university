# University of Cambridge: Undergraduate Application & AI Guidance

- Evidence status: `explicit-undergraduate-ai`
- Research acquisition: source-specific ISO dates below (not publication dates).
- Scope: 本科招生与个人陈述；研究生和在校生作业规则单独区分。

## 中文结论 / Findings

- [S1-Q1] AI 可用于个人陈述准备的构思和研究辅助；页面不是准许直接提交整篇生成文书的承诺。
- [S1-Q2][S1-Q4] 对个人成就的虚假生成主张按申请造假看待；低质量输出也会削弱竞争力。
- [S1-Q3] 无论线上线下面试，期间使用 AI 均被禁止。

**[S1-Q1] 构思/研究辅助**

> You may therefore wish to use AI tools as prompts for ideas or to assist in research when preparing your personal statement, but you should note that an over-reliance on AI carries risks:

**[S1-Q2] 虚构成绩风险**

> If these are about your personal achievements, they will be treated like any other application fraud.

**[S1-Q3] 面试禁止**

> Any use of AI tools during an interview hinders this approach and is therefore prohibited.

**[S1-Q4] 质量风险**

> It may produce poor quality output, weakening your chance of success.
