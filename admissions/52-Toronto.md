**[S2-Q1] admission consequence**

> Students and applicants found to have used generative AI (e.g., ChatGPT) to create content that is presented as their own will be disqualified from eligibility for admission.

**[S2-Q2] prohibition in BAVS/BAAS**

> The use of generative Artificial Intelligence software to produce images or text is not permitted in your application and will result in automatic refusal.
