The thoughts and words contained in your personal statement must be your own and no one else should assist in its creation beyond basic proofreading or critiquing. [S5-Q1]

The personal insight responses should reflect the student’s own ideas and experiences, be independently written by the student and reflect the student’s personal writing style. Students may receive advice on content and editing, including the use of generative artificial intelligence software to assist with readability, but content and final written text must be their own.

[S5-Q2]

UC conducts regular screenings to verify the integrity of the responses, may request authentication of the content or writing as the student’s, and will take action when it is determined that the integrity of the response is compromised.

[S5-Q3]

Students found to have falsified application information will be subject to penalties up to and including denial of admission, withdrawal of an admission offer, registration cancellation, expulsion or revocation of a University of California degree.

中文解读：正式声明允许内容与编辑建议，包括生成式 AI 的可读性辅助；但 PIQ 须体现本人思想、经历和文风，由本人独立写作，内容和最终文字须为本人所有。UC 可要求真实性核验；引文中的处分针对虚假申请信息，不能说成“任何 AI 使用必然受同样处分”。

使用边界（中文解读，不是学校原话）

  • 本科—系统指南提到的辅助：早期构思、整理自己的思路；最终 PIQ 保持本人作品和声音。[S2-Q1]
  • 本科—边界：不能把系统的辅助建议写成允许 AI 代写最终 PIQ；Berkeley 对拼写/语法不单独评分也不等于授权 AI 改写。[S1-Q2][S2-Q1]
  • 本科—未单列：AI 翻译、研究附件、结构或措辞重写阈值、披露要求和处分细节。系统声明的可读性辅助、核验与后果见上方补证；未说明的翻译、具体重写阈值仍不推断。
  • 仅 LL.M.：个人陈述原文限制超出基础校对/评论的他人协助，并明示应不借助生成式 AI 写作；不要据校对措辞自行扩张 AI 许可。[S3-Q1]

Appropriate Use of Generative AI Tools

  • 发布单位Publishing unit Office of Ethics, Risk, and Compliance Services (OERCS)
  • 官方来源Official source https://oercs.berkeley.edu/appropriate-use-generative-ai-tools

The advisory supports innovation while protecting institutional, personal, and proprietary information.

Prohibited use

  • “Completion of academic work in a manner not allowed by the instructor.课程规则优先Instructor override”
  • Unless specifically allowed, “no personal, confidential, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive information may be entered into or generated as output.”数据与隐私Data & privacy
  • Protected examples include FERPA student records, non-public instructional materials, proprietary or unpublished research, and information classified at higher protection levels.知识产权IP & copyright

If considering a new use of generative AI in studies or work, the user is responsible for considering the ethics and risks and must “obtain approval from your instructor/responsible unit head.”默认规则Default rule

UC legal guidance quoted by Berkeley warns that, unless specific precautions are taken, “any content that is provided to generative AI tools can be saved and reused by the company offering the tool.”

Course policy and acknowledgment

  • 发布单位Publishing unit Center for Teaching and Learning
  • 补充官方来源Additional official source https://teaching.berkeley.edu/teaching-strategies/ai-teaching-learning/communicating-course-policies-and-talking-students

Clear course policies should explain what is allowed and why, connect AI rules to learning goals, and support transparency, integrity, and shared responsibility.学习理由Learning rationale When AI is permitted, instructors may require students to include an acknowledgment and share the prompts and outputs used at submission.披露要求Disclosure过程留证Process evidence

The guidance states that GenAI should never be used in a way that would constitute plagiarism if the source were human.摘录Excerpt Having another person or entity write a substantive part of an assignment can include using GenAI tools.违规定性Integrity framing

The page also states that GenAI detection tools are not a viable or recommended enforcement option. Berkeley does not enable Turnitin’s AI detector because of privacy, security, accessibility, effectiveness, and actionability concerns.检测器证据Detector evidence

High-school adaptation takeaways

  • Teacher permission overrides general availability of a tool.
  • Never enter student records, unpublished work, private teaching materials, or other sensitive data into unapproved AI systems.
  • Require a short acknowledgment and, where useful, the prompts and relevant outputs.
  • Do not use AI detectors as automatic proof of misconduct.
