中文结论 / Findings

  • Columbia 本科新生申请页说明：申请人在申请过程中可以使用各种资源、工具和支持；但无论使用何种工具和支持，所有提交的申请材料须为本人原创、体现本人的声音。该页同时提示申请人了解入学后须遵守的 Honor Pledge & Honor Code 与 Generative AI Policy。[S1-Q1]
  • 本科真实性声明要求申请材料准确、真实地代表申请人本人及其成就，提交前须确认同意；提交不完整、不准确或不真实的材料，可能导致拒绝或撤销录取、取消学分、停学、开除或最终撤销学位，申请人可能被要求配合核验。[S2-Q1][S2-Q2]
  • 两页均未逐项列出 AI 的允许或禁止用途，也没有 AI 专门的处分条款，故归为 authenticity-only。S1 提到的 Generative AI Policy 链接至 Columbia 教务长的校内 AI 政策（与本站收录的校内政策为同一来源），适用于入学后的学生，不是申请规则。
  • GSAS 研究生页已核验：须证明材料准确真实，并可能被要求配合核验；不实材料可能导致一系列后果。该页未明确讨论 AI，不外推到本科。[S3]
  • S1/S2 在 2026-09-24 的访问均为 CloudFront 403，当时未取得正文；2026-09-25 在浏览器中正常加载取得正文，据此将证据状态由 no-verified-guidance 更新为 authenticity-only。

  • Students are encouraged to speak with their instructors regarding their expectations.

For Instructors

  • At minimum, it is recommended that faculty share clear expectations at the beginning of each semester through the syllabus, policy distribution, and class discussion on the appropriate use of AI tools. Faculty can encourage students to reach out when they need support rather than risking a potential academic integrity violation. If permitted by the course, encourage students to acknowledge and cite any use of AI applications.披露要求Disclosure
  • It is recommended to develop a course policy about the use of AI tools and what faculty consider to be appropriate and inappropriate in their classes.
  • Important note about AI detection tools: Since the introduction of AI tools, there has been a parallel rise in tools claiming accurate detection of AI-generated work. As with any form of detection software, there are risks of misidentification, which can have consequences in the classroom. These products are best used with careful consideration and as one of many ways to work with students. It is also important to include the use of these tools in any discussion with students around course policies, making clear why and how such services may be used in the course. As with other plagiarism detection tools, AI detection should be treated as a guideline and not a grading metric.检测器证据Detector evidence
