Guidelines for the Georgetown Community on Use of Generative AI

  • 发布单位Publishing unit University Information Services, developed with the Office of General Counsel and university community members
  • 官方来源Official source https://aiguidelines.georgetown.edu

Georgetown recognizes AI’s transformative potential but requires its use to remain aligned with the university’s core principles, institutional values, and existing policies.

The site warns: “Do not upload, input, or analyze university data in an AI tool that is not approved by UIS and under an enterprise-wide contract.数据与隐私Data & privacy机构工具Institutional tools”

Core principles

  • Accountability: “Members of the Georgetown community are accountable for the work product or decisions resulting from their use of AI.摘录Excerpt核实责任Verification duty”
  • Transparency: AI use must be disclosed when required by law, university policy, or publisher requirements.披露要求Disclosure
  • Fairness: Community members should recognize that AI outputs may contain bias.
  • Privacy: Users must respect the privacy of others’ personally identifiable information and applicable law and policy.
  • Security: Users should maintain the security and integrity of Georgetown resources.
  • Intellectual Property and Confidentiality: Users should respect and preserve intellectual-property rights when using AI.知识产权IP & copyright
  • Understanding limitations: Responsible use requires awareness of what AI can and cannot reliably do.

A departmental student example states that students may not use AI tools to complete coursework unless explicitly directed by the instructor, and AI cannot replace the deep learning created by reflection, authentic production, and revision.默认规则Default rule学习理由Learning rationale

High-school adaptation takeaways

  • Organize the school guideline around accountability, transparency, fairness, privacy, security, intellectual property, and limitations.
  • Permit school data only in institutionally approved systems.
  • Keep the student—not the AI provider—accountable for the final work or decision.
  • Explain that permitted AI should not replace reflection, authentic production, and revision.
