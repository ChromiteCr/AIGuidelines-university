中文结论 / Findings

  • 中央本科申请页允许将 AI 作为工具，但最终须为 authentic creation；还要求申请者遵守与在校生相同的相关实践/标准。所链接的学生AI规范未在本轮复核，不补推其具体内容，也不把广义许可扩大为逐项用途清单。[S1-Q1][S1-Q2]
  • 该本科页面声明审阅过程中不使用 AI detection technology；这不等于不核验原创性、真实性，也不保证任何 AI 用法都合规。[S1-Q1][S6]
  • Wagner MSPP 的 ESSAYS 折叠栏现已实际展开并读取：AI 仅可辅助拼写和语法，提交文书须完全本人原创，违反要求学校保留撤录权。这是该研究生项目的更窄要求。[S3]
  • Tisch Film Production MFA 原有 AI 披露与不披露后果保留，仅限该项目。[S4]
  • 中央本科 Application Authenticity 正文也已读取；伪造申请信息的后果不能改写成对一切 AI 使用的处罚。[S6]
  • 2021年署名招生建议只支持真实个人表达，不是当季 AI 专项政策。[S2]

使用边界（中文解读，非原文）

  • 允许／可用： 本科：可以把AI作为工具，但最终提交须为真实原创，不能据此自行列出更细的许可；Wagner MSPP：仅拼写、语法；Tisch MFA：要求披露不等于无条件授权任何用途。[S1][S3][S4]
  • 禁止／不当： 最终材料仍受本人原创/真实性要求约束。Wagner MSPP 明文限定AI为拼写和语法辅助；Tisch MFA 不得隐瞒AI使用；本科虚假信息处分以S6为据，三种要求不混为全校一条禁令。
  • 未说明／未核验： 本科未逐项说明构思、提纲、翻译、润色幅度、短答或补充材料例外、通用披露格式、各工具名单；“不使用AI检测”不等于不核验真实性，也不是不合规使用的保证。

Course permission and academic integrity

NYU’s central student guidance says every course may have different generative-AI rules. Students must check the syllabus and ask the instructor before beginning课程规则优先Instructor override; permission in one class does not carry into another.默认规则Default rule

The central student guidance says that submitting AI-generated content without acknowledgment is plagiarism违规定性Integrity framing and advises students to document their process when AI is permitted过程留证Process evidence.披露要求Disclosure Steinhardt’s narrower guidance likewise treats unattributed AI contributions or AI-generated work presented as one’s own as academic-integrity concerns and points students to NYU’s citation resources.

Verification and responsibility

The central student guidance tells students to verify everything: AI is not a primary source, important factual claims should be corroborated using more than one credible source, and students should keep source notes.核实责任Verification duty Every AI-supplied citation or link should be opened, read, and independently evaluated.摘录Excerpt Submitted work must reflect the student’s own reasoning, voice, and intellectual effort.学习理由Learning rationale

Privacy and intellectual property

NYU’s licensed AI services provide stronger account-level protections than public tools; users should prefer institutionally managed services where appropriate.机构工具Institutional tools Steinhardt specifically warns against submitting student names, NetIDs, N-numbers, or other identifiers to unlicensed third-party services because of FERPA.数据与隐私Data & privacy Users should likewise avoid uploading copyrighted or third-party material without permission.知识产权IP & copyright

High-school adaptation takeaways

  • Re-check AI permission for every course and assignment.
  • Require an AI-use note and, where claims rely on outside information, citations to the original sources rather than merely the chatbot.
  • Open and evaluate every AI-provided link and corroborate important claims.
  • Prefer school-managed accounts and never place student identifiers or records into public tools.
  • Avoid detector-based enforcement; assess drafts, source notes, process evidence, and understanding.
