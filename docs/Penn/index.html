关键发现（中文）

  • Penn 明确说用 AI 组织自己的思路、检查语法不会破坏申请认证；这些帮助必须保留申请人的内容所有权。[S1]
  • 提交不真实属于本人的作品、想法或经历会破坏认证，例子包括他人写文书、AI 生成回答、活动/荣誉虚构。规则覆盖文书、活动、回答及整个申请档案，不是只针对一篇主文书。[S1]
  • 诚信义务不止到提交为止，录取仍以申请时确认的同样标准为条件；页面没有逐项列出 AI 违规对应的自动撤录或开除制裁。[S1]
  • 面向升学顾问的 2026–2027 申请变化页再次确认上述允许边界，并说明 AI 生成推荐信不能替代教师/顾问基于直接了解的判断。[S2]
  • Penn 另说不让 AI 评价材料或作录取决定；AI 可以辅助部分行政工作且有人复核。这是校方流程说明，不是扩大学生 AI 使用权限。[S1][S2]

[S1-Q1]

Penn Admissions asks every applicant to certify that their application is their own work, factually true, and honestly presented.

[S1-Q2]

Getting help is not the same as losing ownership. Talking through an idea with a parent, teacher, or counselor, having someone proofread your writing, or using a tool, including an AI tool, to organize your thoughts or check your grammar, doesn't compromise your application certification.

[S1-Q3]

What would compromise your application is submitting work, ideas, or experiences that aren't authentically yours, whether that means someone else wrote your essay, an AI tool generated your responses, or your activities and honors are embellished or invented.

[S1-Q4]

This same standard extends to every part of your file. Supporting materials must come from you or from an authorized source, and any change in your circumstances or the information you've provided should be reported to us directly, as your certification already requires.

[S1-Q5]

Your offer of admission remains contingent on the same standards you affirmed when you applied.

[S2-Q1]

Just as talking through an idea with a parent, teacher, or counselor or having someone proofread your writing are permissible, so is using an AI to organize your thoughts or check your grammar.

[S2-Q2]

Counselors’ and teachers’ judgement and direct, detailed knowledge of students cannot be replaced by AI-generated letters of recommendation and evaluations.

[S2-Q3]

Penn does not use AI to evaluate materials or to make decisions about admissions.

中文说明：本页标题下明确是 What’s Changing for Penn’s 2026-2027 Application；申请季不是发布日期，故 page_date 仍为 not stated。引文分别对应申请人、推荐人和校方，不能混作一个统一的“AI 可用/不可用”结论。

Permitted / prohibited / not stated（按用途）

  • 明确允许：用 AI 组织自己的思路、检查语法；真人讨论想法或校对同样被认可，但申请内容仍须属于本人。[S1][S2]
  • 不符合认证：AI 生成申请回答、他人代写、提交非本人真实想法/经历、虚构或夸大活动荣誉。[S1]
  • 未单独说明：AI 翻译、生成提纲与“组织思路”的具体界线、逐句重写、科研补充材料专门规则、AI 使用披露格式、AI 检测工具和具体制裁表。不能把“组织思路”扩张为任意生成内容的许可。
  • 推荐人另行适用：直接了解学生的判断与细节不可被 AI 生成信件替代；这不是一条已经明确禁止所有推荐信 AI 语法辅助的规定。[S2]
  • 后果 / 持续义务：申请认证及诚实要求贯穿后续，录取以遵守同样标准为条件；未查到本文来源列举的专门 AI 自动惩罚。[S1]

Wharton Global Youth Program AI Policy

  • 官方来源Official source https://globalyouth.wharton.upenn.edu/ai-policy/

  • RelevanceRelevance This is an especially useful university-affiliated model for high-school students.

Ethics

All Wharton Global High School Investment competition team members are held to high personal ethical standards. The decisions you make from the moment you register for the competition should be honest and truthful to the best of your ability. This starts with your number of team members (you must have at least four active members at all times to compete) and ends with how you articulate your strategy and investment choices in your final papers — and includes everything in between. We trust that you will not lie about your investment decisions and outcomes, fabricate analyses, invent teamwork and experiential stories, compensate advisors, education consultants, or other agents, enroll in non-Wharton extracurricular courses that claim to “teach” the competition, pass AI generated writing or work off as your own, or plagiarize existing strategies. If at any point you are unsure about a decision or situation, please reach out to the Wharton Global Youth Program team for clarification.

Students must abide by the University of Pennsylvania’s Code of Academic Integrity, which states a student’s work must be their own, and not be plagiarized from any other source, including advisors or “unofficial” advisors. Plagiarism includes, but is not limited to, the use of another’s words or ideas as if they are one’s own. Plagiarism and any kind of academic cheating are grounds for dismissal from the competition. If at any point you are unsure about a decision or situation, please reach out to the Wharton Global Youth Program staff for clarification.
