[S2-Q1] Undergraduate FAQ policy: brainstorm/organize, not content creation

As part of our contextual review at Rice, we value your authentic voice and story above all else. AI can be a helpful tool for brainstorming or organizing your ideas, much like consulting teachers, counselors, or family members. However, preserving your original thoughts remains essential. In alignment with Rice’s commitment to responsible AI usage, any resource you use should ultimately amplify your voice and should not create content for you, as no one else can articulate your unique lived experiences and perspective.

High-school adaptation takeaways

  • Establish a default rule but allow teachers to set clearer assignment-specific conditions.
  • Treat passing off AI-generated ideas—not only copied wording—as plagiarism.
  • Use drafts and editing history as process evidence.
  • AI-detector output may trigger a conversation but must not serve as sole or primary proof.
