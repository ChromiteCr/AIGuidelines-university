关键发现（中文）

  • 本科申请文书应以自己的话介绍自己，并保留真实声音；这是可核验的真实性要求，不是逐项列出的 AI 禁用或许可清单。[S1]
  • 在本科文书页、申请状态 FAQ 及定向检索范围内，未取得明确说明 AI 头脑风暴、语法检查、翻译或生成文书的本科专门规则。不能把未找到解释为许可。[S1][S2]
  • 人文与科学学院的研究生申请指南明确提醒慎用生成式 AI，以免陈述不忠于本人经历；其措辞是审慎建议，不是全面禁止，也没有“只允许头脑风暴”的清单。[S3]
  • GSB MBA 文书规则禁止由另一人或工具代写，可能拒绝申请或撤销录取；允许他人指出错误，由申请人自己修改并保留思想、声音与风格。这不是本科规则，也不是禁止一切 AI 辅助。[S4]

Generative AI Policy Guidance

  • 官方来源Official source https://communitystandards.stanford.edu/policies-guidance%23policies-guidance-links/bca-guidance-recommendations
  • 发布机构Issuing body Stanford Office of Community Standards / Board on Conduct Affairs (BCA)
  • Guidance adoptedGuidance adopted 2023-02-16

To give sufficient space for instructors to explore uses of generative AI tools in their courses, and to set clear guidelines to students about what uses are and are not consistent with the Stanford Honor Code, the BCA has set forth policy guidance regarding generative AI in the context of coursework.违规定性Integrity framing

As part of the BCA’s guidance on clear communication of a course’s generative AI policy, OCS recommends course instructors provide clear advance notice that they may use detection software to review work submitted for use of generative AI.检测器证据Detector evidence

If you are in doubt about whether a generative AI source (or any source) is permitted aid in the context of a particular assignment, talk with the instructor.

The Board on Conduct Affairs (BCA) has been asked to address the Honor Code implications of generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Bard, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion. These are novel tools, and both students and instructors have been experimenting with their use in academic settings.

While these tools have applications that foster student learning and understanding, these tools can also be used in ways that bypass key learning objectives.学习理由Learning rationale

To give sufficient space for instructors to explore uses of generative AI tools in their courses, and to set clear guidelines to students about what uses are and are not consistent with the Stanford Honor Code, the BCA has set forth the following policy guidance regarding generative AI in the context of coursework:

Absent a clear statement from a course instructor, use of or consultation with generative AI shall be treated analogously to assistance from another person.默认规则Default rule摘录Excerpt In particular, using generative AI tools to substantially complete an assignment or exam (e.g. by entering exam or assignment questions) is not permitted. Students should acknowledge the use of generative AI (other than incidental use) and default to disclosing such assistance when in doubt.披露要求Disclosure分级许可Tiered scale

Individual course instructors are free to set their own policies regulating the use of generative AI tools in their courses, including allowing or disallowing some or all uses of such tools.课程规则优先Instructor override Course instructors should set such policies in their course syllabi and clearly communicate such policies to students. Students who are unsure of policies regarding generative AI tools are encouraged to ask their instructors for clarification.

The BCA will continue to monitor developments in these tools and their use in academic settings and may update this guidance. Members of the community are encouraged to contact the BCA to provide input, suggestions, and comments on this policy.
