中文结论（解读，非原文）

  • UCLA 本科 PIQ 页强调个人声音和具体经历，但没有直接讨论 AI；不能把人工反馈/拼写检查建议自动换成 AI 许可。[S1]
  • 可核验的本科 AI 建议来自 UC 系统 2026–27 指南：早期构思/整理可以获得辅助，最终 PIQ 保留本人作品和声音。作者是 UC 系统，非 UCLA 独有政策。[S2]
  • UCLA 国际本科页要求申请是学生本人的作品，并列出可能的拒收材料、撤销/取消录取或强制退学等后果；此处是一般真实性要求，不是 AI 专项处罚。[S4-Q1]
  • 单独的 Anderson 全日制 MBA FAQ 允许语法、拼写和清晰度编辑，但内容、思想、写作风格必须属于申请者；AI 不应生成、重写或显著改变实质内容。不可外推本科。[S3-Q1]

These questions are about getting to know you better, so be open, reflective, find your individual voice and express it.

[S3-Q1] Full-Time MBA only: grammar/clarity editing permitted, substance generation/rewriting restricted

Authenticity is essential in your application essays. While AI can assist in refining essays, applicants must ensure their final work represents their originality and creativity. Applicants may use tools to edit and proof their essays for grammar, spelling, and clarity, but the content, ideas, and writing style must be their own. AI should not generate, rewrite, or significantly alter the essay’s substance. The admissions team values genuine, personal reflections that showcase each applicant’s unique voice.

[S6-Q1]

The personal insight responses should reflect the student’s own ideas and experiences, be independently written by the student and reflect the student’s personal writing style. Students may receive advice on content and editing, including the use of generative artificial intelligence software to assist with readability, but content and final written text must be their own.

[S6-Q2]

UC conducts regular screenings to verify the integrity of the responses, may request authentication of the content or writing as the student’s, and will take action when it is determined that the integrity of the response is compromised.

[S6-Q3]

Students found to have falsified application information will be subject to penalties up to and including denial of admission, withdrawal of an admission offer, registration cancellation, expulsion or revocation of a University of California degree.

中文解读：正式声明允许内容与编辑建议，包括生成式 AI 的可读性辅助；但 PIQ 须体现本人思想、经历和文风，由本人独立写作，内容和最终文字须为本人所有。UC 可要求真实性核验；引文中的处分针对虚假申请信息，不能说成“任何 AI 使用必然受同样处分”。

使用边界（中文解读，不是学校原话）

  • 本科—明确的系统 AI 辅助：早期构思、整理思路，最终 PIQ 保持本人作品和声音。[S2-Q1]
  • 本科—一般本人作品要求：国际申请页面将偏离本人作品要求列为违反政策并可能带来后果；不是 AI 检测即自动处罚。[S4-Q1]
  • 本科—未单独说明：AI 翻译、研究附件、编辑深度、披露/记录以及检测器使用。
  • 仅全日制 MBA：允许工具做语法、拼写、清晰度编辑；AI 不应生成、重写或大幅改变内容实质。[S3-Q1]

限制与未说明事项

  • S2 是 UC 系统作者的 first-year 指南；不得在网站上标成 UCLA 独有 AI 政策。
  • S4 的明确真实性条款位于国际本科申请/代理与顾问栏目，不是独立 AI 政策；后果为 may lead to，不能写成自动拒绝。
  • S1 的人工反馈、修订和拼写检查建议没有明文许可 AI；不以人工许可替代 AI 规则。
  • UCLA 本科 AI 翻译、研究补充材料、深度润色、披露/留档或 AI 检测程序未在这些校园来源逐项说明。
  • 已并列补入 UC 公开申请诚信声明；本人写作、可读性辅助和一般虚假申请后果以该声明为据，不以系统指南简句推导全部后果。

AI Use and Recommendation Guide

  • 发布单位Publishing unit UCLA Digital & Technology Solutions (DTS)
  • 官方来源Official source https://dts.ucla.edu/initiatives/ai/ai-use-recommendation-guide

UCLA supports “the responsible, transparent, ethical, and risk-informing use of generative AI tools” in education, research, and administration.

Data protection

Most approved tools are restricted to low-risk data, and users must check each tool’s permitted data classification.机构工具Institutional tools Unless explicitly approved in a secure environment, users may not enter:数据与隐私Data & privacy

  • FERPA-protected student information, including IDs, grades, and coursework;
  • health or personnel information;
  • “Unpublished research, IP, or grant proposals”;知识产权IP & copyright
  • export-controlled or restricted data; or
  • malicious or harmful prompts.

Academic integrity

“Students may not present AI-generated work as their own without acknowledgement.披露要求Disclosure摘录Excerpt Misuse constitutes plagiarism under the UCLA Student Conduct Code.默认规则Default rule违规定性Integrity framing”

Reflective and responsible use in teaching and learning

  • 发布单位Publishing unit UCLA Teaching and Learning Center
  • 补充官方来源Additional official source https://teaching.ucla.edu/resources/teaching-guides/using-generative-ai-reflectively-and-responsibly-in-teaching-and-learning

The resource advises instructors to state their GenAI policy explicitly. A no-use model treats confirmed use of AI writing tools as academic dishonesty.课程规则优先Instructor override A limited-use model requires AI-generated content to be reviewed, edited, and verified for accuracy before submission.分级许可Tiered scale核实责任Verification duty

When students present a significant amount of minimally edited AI-generated text, the guidance says they should identify the specific tool and follow appropriate citation guidance.

High-school adaptation takeaways

  • Distinguish approved school AI platforms from consumer accounts.
  • Students must acknowledge AI-generated material and cannot present it as their own.
  • Require review, editing, fact-checking, and citation when AI use is permitted.
  • Keep FERPA-like student records, health information, unpublished research, and intellectual property out of unapproved systems.
