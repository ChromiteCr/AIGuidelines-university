52 所大学 AI 使用政策比较研究

AI Use Policies at 52 Universities: A Comparative Study

本研究采集 52 所大学公开发布的 AI 使用政策，按 12 项条款逐校编码比较，并以其中美国 30 所的比较结果为依据，制定面向国际部高中生的《学生 AI 使用规范》；另整理 52 所大学招生办公室对申请人使用 AI 的规定。 图谱中的每项编码、规范中的每个数据，均可追溯至官方原文。

This study collects the published AI use policies of 52 universities, codes each against 12 provisions, and on the comparison of the 30 U.S. universities among them sets out Student AI Use Guidelines for international-division high-school students. It also compiles what the admissions offices of all 52 say about applicants using AI. Every coding in the atlas and every figure in the guidelines traces back to official text.

核验方法

Verification

Traceability
01
规范中的数字与数据集比对Figures in the guidelines are checked against the dataset

规范中的每一处数字断言在构建时与数据集比对，每条引文与其来源文件逐字比对，不一致则构建中止。当前 98 处断言、9 条引文全部通过。

Every numeric claim in the guidelines is checked against the dataset at build time, and every quotation against its source file; a mismatch stops the build. Currently 98 assertions and 9 quotations pass.

02
逐校隔离抽取，独立审计复核Isolated extraction, independent audit

52 份来源文件由 52 个相互隔离的程序分别抽取，每个程序只读取对应学校的文件，不得使用关于该校的既有知识；随后由独立审计重读同一文件复核。

The 52 source files were read by 52 isolated passes, each limited to its own university's file and barred from prior knowledge of that institution, then re-checked by an independent audit against the same file.

03
每条引文逐字比对Each quotation is matched verbatim

所有引文与其所属文件做精确字符串比对；未匹配的编码降为“未提及”并记录，出现在其他学校文件中的引文标记为串源。当前 388/388 通过。

Every quotation is matched exactly against the file it claims to come from; an unmatched coding is demoted to "not addressed" and logged, and a quotation found in another university's file is flagged as cross-contamination. Currently 388/388 pass.

因此，任何一项编码都可以对照原文复核，如有误判欢迎指正。完整方法与局限见图谱「方法与局限」。

Any coding can therefore be checked against the source, and corrections are welcome. The full method and its limitations are set out in the atlas under "方法与局限" (Method & limitations).
