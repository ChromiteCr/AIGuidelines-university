**[S2-Q1]**

> Before doing so, double check your proposed use against your professor's AI policies, the assignment requirements, and the Honor Code. What may be acceptable use in one class may be in direct violation of another professor's policies.


**[S2-Q2]**

> We encourage you to find and read the privacy policies of any tools you plan to use and to be careful about the information that you share with them.
