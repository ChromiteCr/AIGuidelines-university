# Carleton College: Academic/Student AI Guidance

- Research record: official source snapshots acquired at the exact UTC times listed below; reviewed for the 2026-09-26 college-fair expansion.
- Scope: academic/coursework; academic course rules and undergraduate-admission rules must not be interchanged.
- Evidence status: `university-wide-policy`. This is a classification of verified evidence, not a permission rating.

## 中文结论 / Findings

- Carleton College校园手册的学术诚信政策要求课程作业的核心智力内容由本人完成；可用哪些外部资源（包括生成式AI）取决于教师与具体作业。[S2-Q1][S2-Q2]
- 即使合作或外部工具获准，也须承认其使用；这不等于只要披露就可以使用。[S2-Q3]
- 校园AI政策汇总页指出该手册是权威学术诚信全文；页面中针对雇员的数据输入规则不能直接当作所有学生或本科申请者的条款。[S1-Q1]

- 名称说明：本档案为美国明尼苏达州 Carleton College，并非加拿大 Carleton University。
