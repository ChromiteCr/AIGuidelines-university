# National University of Singapore: Student/academic AI guidance

- Status: `university-wide-policy` (see scope of each source; not a blanket university-wide rule).
- 中文结论：NUS 2026年教学政策规定无监考任务默认可在充分声明后使用AI，并禁止把AI检测器判定直接作为纪律证据；学生指南要求按课程目标与教师要求判断许可，声明使用并核查输出。此条不适用于申请文书。
- 原文引句与中文概述分开；原文来自保存的官方正文，经空白归一化对照。检索未发现规定不意味着允许使用。

**[S1-Q1] unsupervised assessment default**

> Conversely, the default assumption for any unsupervised (e.g., “take-home”) assessment task is that the use of AI tools is permitted so long as the use is duly acknowledged.

**[S1-Q2] AI detector verdicts**

> The verdicts of current AI tools purporting to determine whether an analysed input has been generated by AI are not admissible as evidence in a disciplinary process to charge a student with academic dishonesty or as justification to penalize student work.

**[S1-Q3] unacknowledged output**

> Given the above, representing an AI’s output as your own work , without any acknowledgement that you have used such a tool , is plagiarism .

**[S2-Q1] course discretion**

> Whether or not using an AI tool in a particular way is allowable depends on the learning purposes of the course and the targeted outcomes of the assignment.

**[S2-Q2] disclosure**

> If you completed any work with the aid of an AI tool, assuming a setting in which the instructor gave permission for such tools to be used, you should always acknowledge the use.

**[S2-Q3] verification**

> You should thus always assume that the AI’s output is incorrect until you have separately checked it against reliable sources (citing those sources properly) or have gone through the workings yourself.

## 适用边界

- 教学和课程作业来源不可当作本科招生规则；具体教师或项目要求优先单独查证。

- 2026-09-15教学政策中的无监考评估默认许可仅适用于教学与评估，不适用于招生文书；监督评估和不同任务的要求分别核对。
