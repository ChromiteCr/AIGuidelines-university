- Evidence status: `university-wide-policy`
- Research acquisition: source-specific ISO dates below (not publication dates).

## 中文结论 / Findings

- [X3-Q1] 学习、研究、形成性工作可适当使用 AI，但不能直接推导总结性评估可用。
- [X3-Q2] 未披露 AI 生成内容并冒作本人在总结性评估中提交，除任务说明另有规定外属学术不端。披露不自动授权作业中已被禁止的使用。
- [X3-Q3] 具体疑问询问指导教师或讲师。

**[X3-Q1] 个人学习许可**

> Students are permitted to make appropriate use of artificial intelligence tools to support their personal study, research and formative work.

**[X3-Q2] 总结性评估不端**

> A student using any unacknowledged content generated by artificial intelligence within a summative assessment as though it is their own work constitutes academic misconduct, unless explicitly stated otherwise in the assessment brief.

**[X3-Q3] 询问导师**

> If you have any concerns regarding the potential use of artificial intelligence, please discuss this with your supervisor or lecturer

## High-school adaptation (researcher recommendation; not university policy)

- 每项课程、项目或评估先查教师书面规则；文书由申请人真实反思并亲自核对事实。
- 明确区分可用于构思或校对、可提交的内容与不可在面试／评估使用的工具；勿将大学在校作业规则照搬入高中或本科申请。
