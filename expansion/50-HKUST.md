**[S1-Q1] verification**

> Always verify and fact-check AI-generated outputs carefully, as generative AI may produce inaccurate or fabricated information ("hallucinations").

**[S1-Q2] acknowledgement**

> When appropriate, acknowledge or label AI-generated content to maintain transparency and uphold academic integrity.

**[S1-Q3] privacy**

> Avoid inputting confidential, sensitive, or personal data into generative AI tools.
