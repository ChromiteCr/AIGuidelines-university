§0

为什么会有这份规范

Why this document exists

你申请的那些大学，已经各自写下了规则。

本规范的依据是一项文本比较研究：采集三十所美国大学公开发布的 AI 使用政策，拆解为 12 项可比条款，逐项编码后对照。在「作业未说明可否使用 AI 时适用何种规则」这一最基础的 问题上，二十九所在其官方来源中作出明确表述的学校分为四种口径。

默认规则 · 一致度 45% 13 所默认禁止未经许可的使用 · 5 所将其等同于他人代为完成 · 3 所有条件允许 · 8 所交由任课教师逐门确定 · 1 所未作表述

该分布表明，各校在此问题上尚未形成统一口径。因此本规范并非对某一权威做法的照搬，而是在 上述分歧中作出的选择。每项选择的依据、以及未被采纳的其他口径，均在正文 中标明，以便核查。

§6.2 涉及大学申请文书。该节所述后果与其余各节不在同一量级：课程层面的认定影响一门课程的 成绩与校内纪律记录，申请层面的认定可能影响录取资格。

The universities you are applying to have each written their own rules.

This document rests on a comparative reading of published policy text: the AI use policies of thirty U.S. universities, broken into twelve comparable provisions and coded provision by provision. On the most basic question — what applies when an assignment says nothing about AI — the twenty-nine that state a position in their official sources divide four ways.

Default rule · 45% agreement 13 prohibit unapproved use by default · 5 treat it as work done by another person · 3 allow it conditionally · 8 leave it to the instructor · 1 states no position

That distribution shows no settled position across the thirty. This document is therefore not a copy of any single authority, but a set of choices made among those differences. The reasoning for each, and the alternatives not adopted, are stated in the text so that both can be checked.

§6.2 concerns college application essays. Its consequences are not of the same order as the rest: a finding on coursework affects a course grade and an internal disciplinary record; a finding on an application can affect an offer of admission.

§1

四档许可框架

Four permission levels

三十所学校里，只有三所给出了明确的分级框架——Johns Hopkins 三档、UNC 四档、Brown 五档。 另有十所用非正式的分类，十七所完全没提。本校采用四档，命名为 AI-0 到 AI-3，短到可以印在作业单的角落。

Only three of the thirty publish an explicit graduated framework — Johns Hopkins with three levels, UNC with four, Brown with five. Ten more use informal categories; seventeen say nothing. We use four, labelled AI-0 through AI-3, short enough to print in the corner of an assignment sheet.

AI-0
不可使用No AI

全程不得使用，包括查词与语法检查。

No use at any stage, including dictionaries and grammar checkers.

限时写作 · 语言能力测评 · 考试 Timed writing · language assessments · exams
AI-1
仅限辅助Assistive only

可以使用，但不得产生新内容：查词、拼写与语法纠错、格式排版、 作为检索的起点、请它解释你看不懂的概念。

Permitted, but it may not produce new content: dictionaries, spelling and grammar correction, formatting, a starting point for searching, explaining a concept you don't understand.

多数常规作业的默认可选档 The usual level for routine work
AI-2
可生成，须改造并披露Generative, with rework and disclosure

AI 可以产出内容，但你必须实质改造它、核实它，按 §3 披露， 并附上未经 AI 修改的原稿。

AI may produce content, but you must substantially rework it, verify it, disclose under §3, and attach your un-assisted draft.

研究性作业 · 编程 · 数据分析 Research tasks · programming · data analysis
AI-3
以 AI 为对象AI as the subject

作业本身要求你使用、比较或评价 AI 的输出。仍须披露与核实。

The assignment itself asks you to use, compare, or critique AI output. Disclosure and verification still apply.

专门设计的探究任务 Purpose-built inquiry tasks
判准The test

这件事产生新内容吗？产生 → 至少 AI-2。不产生 → AI-1 够了。

Does this produce new content? If yes → AI-2 at minimum. If no → AI-1 covers it.

本规范不为各档位提供穷举清单。工具与用法持续变化，清单无法保持 完备，而不完备的清单容易被解释为「未列出即允许」。判断依据为上述单一判准，具体情形见 附录 B。

This document publishes no exhaustive list per level. Tools and uses keep changing, so no list stays complete, and an incomplete list tends to be read as permission for whatever it omits. The single test above governs; concrete situations are in Appendix B.

AI-1 之所以成立，芝加哥大学说得最清楚：

The University of Chicago states the reasoning behind AI-1 most clearly:

The principle here is that if generative AI is performing a task that other technologies (or people) currently perform, and such uses are not a violation of academic integrity, then those uses of generative AI do not violate the default policy.

University of Chicago Law School · univ/10-UChicago.md
§2

默认规则与标级

The default, and how levels get assigned

本校规定This school's rule

默认 AI-0。任课教师可以为具体作业放宽到任一更高档位。

AI-0 by default. Teachers may raise the level for any specific assignment.

未标注档位的作业，按 AI-0 处理。如果你不确定某份作业属于哪一档， 你有权在动手之前直接问，而不是事后被追究。

An assignment with no level marked is AI-0. If you are unsure which level applies, you are entitled to ask before you start rather than be judged after you finish.

采用该默认值的理由是可预测性，而非严格程度。将默认规则交由任课教师逐门 确定，在大学环境中可行——三十所中有八所学校就是这么规定的——其前提是教师普遍会在课程说明中 写明 AI 条款。该前提在高中并不稳定成立。当默认规则为「由教师确定」而教师未作说明时，规则 处于空缺状态：使用可能构成违规，不使用则可能在同等要求下处于不利位置。以 AI-0 为默认值 可消除该空缺——未获明确许可即不得使用，需要使用则事先询问。

The reason for this default is predictability, not severity. Leaving the default to each instructor is workable at university — eight of the thirty rely on exactly that — because instructors there generally publish an AI clause in the course policy. That condition does not hold reliably in high school. Where the default is "the instructor decides" and no instructor has decided, the rule is simply absent: using AI may be a violation, and not using it may leave you at a disadvantage under the same requirements. An AI-0 default removes that gap — without explicit permission you may not use it, and if you need to, you ask first.

当你拿不准某个用法算不算过线时，斯坦福给了一把最好用的尺子：

When you cannot tell whether a particular use crosses the line, Stanford offers the most useful yardstick:

Absent a clear statement from a course instructor, use of or consultation with generative AI shall be treated analogously to assistance from another person.

Stanford Board on Conduct Affairs · univ/01-Stanford.md

该类比的作用，是把一个缺乏经验的判断转换成一个已有判断标准的问题。多数人难以判断 「AI 参与到何种程度构成违规」，但对「由他人代写这一段是否可接受」 有稳定的判断。斯坦福的表述要求沿用后一判断。

The purpose of the analogy is to convert an unfamiliar judgment into a familiar one. Few people can say how much AI involvement crosses the line, but almost everyone has a settled view on whether it would be acceptable for a classmate to write that paragraph. Stanford's formulation asks you to apply the second judgment to the first question.

§3

披露怎么做

How to disclose

披露要求 · 一致度 43% Disclosure · 43% agreement 12 所强制 · 1 所存疑即披露 · 6 所建议 · 9 所依课程而定 · 2 所未提及 12 require it · 1 says disclose when in doubt · 6 recommend it · 9 leave it to the course · 2 are silent
本校规定This school's rule

AI-1 及以上，一律须披露。三行，附在作业末尾。

Disclosure is required at AI-1 and above. Three lines at the end of your work.

采用强制而非建议，理由是执行成本：大学单门课程规模常达数百人，高中班级约三十人，逐份查阅 披露声明在后者可行。可执行性是规则能否实际生效的前提。

Required rather than recommended, on grounds of enforcement cost: a university lecture may hold several hundred students, a class here about thirty, and reading thirty statements is feasible. Enforceability is a precondition for a rule taking effect at all. 

AI 使用声明
工具：ChatGPT (GPT-5)
环节：初稿构思；语法检查
程度：AI-1 —— 仅用于语法纠错，未生成任何段落
AI Use Statement
Tool:    ChatGPT (GPT-5)
Where:   brainstorming the outline; grammar check
Extent:  AI-1 — grammar correction only; no text generated

三件套：什么工具、用在哪一步、用到什么程度。这三项分别有 9 所、14 所、 14 所学校要求，是跨校统计出的最小集合。采用固定格式而非自由撰写，是为降低填写成本：结构化 表单只需补全字段，开放式说明则要求使用者自行决定披露到何种程度。

Three things: which tool, at which step, to what extent. Nine, fourteen, and fourteen of the thirty require these respectively — this is the minimum set the data supports. The format is fixed to lower the cost of complying: a structured form only asks you to fill in fields, whereas an open-ended statement requires you to decide for yourself how much to say.

提示词日志只在 AI-2 及以上要求。八所学校要求记录 prompt。但若全班每份作业 均附提示词记录，实际上无法逐份查阅；长期不被查阅的要求会失去约束力，并连带削弱本规范其余 条款的可信度。

Prompt logs are required only at AI-2 and above. Eight of the thirty ask for prompts. But if every student attaches one to every assignment, none of them can actually be read; a requirement that is never checked loses force, and weakens the credibility of the rest of this document with it.

最重要的一条The one that matters most

披露不等于获得许可。一份标为 AI-0 的作业，你如实声明用了 AI， 仍然是违规。声明治不好一个本就被禁止的用法。

Disclosing is not the same as being allowed. On an AI-0 assignment, an honest statement that you used AI is still a violation. Disclosure does not cure a use that was prohibited to begin with.

A student cannot avoid limits on the use of generative AI that would otherwise apply by attributing their work to generative AI. Under the default policy, using generative AI to compose all or part of a paper, even if the use of generative AI is fully documented in properly placed footnotes, is a violation.

University of Chicago · univ/10-UChicago.md

华盛顿大学圣路易斯与南加州大学各有一句同义的规定。这一点上没有分歧。

WashU and USC each state the same rule in their own words. There is no disagreement on this point.

§4

你要负的责任

What you are responsible for

一 · 核实

1 · Verification

本校规定This school's rule

你对交上来的成品负 100% 的责任。编造的引注、错误的史实、 幻觉出来的数据、抄错的公式——全部算在你头上。

You are 100% responsible for what you submit. Invented citations, wrong facts, hallucinated data, a mis-copied formula — all of it lands on you.

这是三十校中分歧最大的一条（一致度 39%），但在高中场景下取向较为明确。该条款的作用是排除 一类免责主张，即以「内容由 AI 生成」为由推卸对错误的责任。若不作明确规定，本规范其余条款 均可被「不知其为误」的说法规避。 用的是北卡罗来纳大学的口径——该校要求学生对最终成品负 "ultimately 100% responsible" 的责任，并把它和另一句话放在一起：

This is the most contested provision in the set (39% agreement), though in a high-school setting the direction is fairly clear. Its function is to rule out one class of defence: disclaiming responsibility for an error on the ground that AI produced it. Without it, every other rule here can be evaded with "I did not know it was wrong." The wording follows UNC's, which holds students "ultimately 100% responsible" for the final product and pairs that with a second line:

AI should help you think, not think for you.

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill · univ/25-UNC-Chapel-Hill.md

二 · 隐私与数据

2 · Privacy and data

数据与隐私 · 一致度 72%（可评级条款中最高） Data & privacy · 72% agreement, the highest of any gradable provision 13 所禁止上传受限数据 · 2 所视上传为公开 · 3 所一般提醒 · 12 所未提及 13 prohibit uploading restricted data · 2 treat upload as public disclosure · 3 give general caution · 12 are silent
本校不指定 AI 工具 This school designates no AI tool

本校不提供机构版工具，也不发布批准工具清单。这是一个明确的选择， 它的代价必须说清楚：这一节的责任完整地落在你身上，没有任何豁免口。

We provide no institutional tier and publish no approved-tool list. That is a deliberate choice, and its cost has to be stated plainly: everything in this section rests on you, with no exemption.

具体意味着三件事。其一，下面那份禁止清单没有例外情形—— 别的学校可以说「用批准的工具就可以」，本校说不了这句话。 其二，你不能依赖任何工具的隐私承诺：本校没有审查过任何一家的条款， 也无法为其背书。其三，关掉「用于训练」的开关不等于内容没有被存储、 被缓存、或被人工审阅。

Three consequences. First, the prohibition list below has no carve-out — other schools can say "it's fine on an approved tool"; we cannot. Second, you cannot rely on any vendor's privacy promise, because nobody here has reviewed one and this school vouches for none of them. Third, switching off "use my data for training" does not mean the content was not stored, cached, or read by a person.

这个选择本身并不异常——三十所学校里有十九所同样没有指定任何工具。但值得注意的是， 在表态的十一所里，七所提供机构版工具、四所发布批准清单，理由都是数据保护。 我们不做这件事，所以那份保护得由你自己提供。

The choice is not unusual — nineteen of the thirty likewise designate nothing. What is worth noticing is that among the eleven that do address it, seven provide an institutional tier and four publish an approved list, and in every case the reason is data protection. We are not doing that, so the protection has to come from you.

不得输入 AI 工具的内容 Never put these into an AI tool

他人的个人信息与身份识别信息（姓名、学号、联系方式）· 同学的作业或作品 · 未公开的试题与答案 · 身份证件、成绩单、账号密码 · 教师未公开的材料 · 任何你无权对外公开的东西

Other people's personal and identifying information (names, student numbers, contact details) · a classmate's work · unreleased exam papers or answers · ID documents, transcripts, credentials · unpublished teacher materials · anything you are not free to publish yourself

伯克利把这条边界写得最简洁：

Berkeley draws the boundary most concisely:

no personal, confidential, proprietary, or otherwise sensitive information may be entered into or generated as output.

University of California, Berkeley · univ/16-UC-Berkeley.md

而在没有任何工具经过审查的前提下，加州理工那把尺子就不是补充建议， 而是本节的操作规则：

And with no tool here vetted by anyone, Caltech's yardstick is not supplementary advice — it is the working rule of this section:

Unless you are using a GenAI application that explicitly guarantees data separation and confidentiality, uploading information into GenAI tools should be considered a public disclosure.

California Institute of Technology · univ/12-Caltech.md

应将任何上传视同公开发布。提交前的判断标准是：该内容若出现在公开网页上 是否可接受。不可接受，则不应输入。

Treat any upload as publication. The test before submitting is whether the content would be acceptable on a public web page. If it would not, it should not be entered.

三 · 版权

3 · Copyright

不得上传或转发受版权保护的材料、教师自制课件、购买的教辅、未获授权的电子书。 在三十所学校里，提到这一条的十七所全部有明确规定，没有一所例外—— 这是整批数据里唯一零分歧的条款。

Do not upload or redistribute copyrighted material, a teacher's own slides, purchased workbooks, or unlicensed e-books. Of the thirty, seventeen address this and all seventeen have an explicit rule — the only provision in the entire dataset with zero disagreement.

§5

被质疑的时候

If your work is questioned

违规如何处理What a violation means

按现有的学术诚信制度处理，本规范不另设一套惩戒办法。 违规使用 AI 与抄袭、代做走同一条通道，适用同样的程序与后果。

Through the existing academic-integrity process. This document creates no separate penalty system. A violation here travels the same route as plagiarism or submitting someone else's work, with the same procedure and the same consequences.

这一点上三十所学校几乎没有分歧：二十七所把违规使用定性为学术诚信问题，其中十六所归入学术 不端、九所明确等同抄袭。没有一所为 AI 另建一套制度。

The thirty barely disagree here: twenty-seven classify unauthorized use as an academic-integrity matter — sixteen as misconduct, nine as plagiarism outright. Not one builds a separate regime for AI.

Plagiarism includes, but is not limited to, the unauthorized use of generative artificial intelligence to create content that is submitted as one's own.

Northwestern University · univ/09-Northwestern.md

本节其余部分规定认定程序中的证据标准。仅规定学生义务而不规定认定方式的文件，其约束是单向的； 载明举证要求之后，双方才受同一套规则约束。

The rest of this section sets the evidentiary standard for a finding. A document that states only student obligations binds one side; once it also states what must be shown, both sides are held to the same rules.

本校规定This school's rule

AI 检测器的结果不得作为认定的唯一或主要依据。 它可以触发一次对话，不能单独定性。

An AI-detector score may not be the sole or primary basis for a finding. It can start a conversation; it cannot end one.

检测器证据 · 三十校中仅 11 所有表态 Detector evidence · only 11 of 30 take a position 7 所明确「不得作为唯一或主要证据」（哥伦比亚、杜克、范德堡、伯克利、 莱斯、弗吉尼亚、南加州）· 3 所质疑其可靠性 · 1 所要求提前告知 · 19 所完全沉默 7 state it may not be sole or primary evidence (Columbia, Duke, Vanderbilt, Berkeley, Rice, UVA, USC) · 3 question its reliability · 1 requires advance notice · 19 say nothing at all

Current guidelines indicate that a report to the Undergraduate Honor Council cannot be based solely on an artificial intelligence detector score.

Vanderbilt University · univ/15-Vanderbilt.md

该条款对本校学生具有特殊意义。范德堡在同一份文件中指出，检测工具对 非母语英语写作者存在系统性偏差。二语学习者的书面表达通常具有若干共同特征： 词汇选择趋于保守、句式结构较为规整、较多使用课堂习得的固定搭配。这些特征与检测工具用以 判定「疑似机器生成」的指标高度重合，但其反映的是语言学习的阶段性状态，而非文本的实际来源。

This provision carries particular weight for students here. Vanderbilt's own guidance records that detection tools are systematically biased against non-native English writers. Second-language writing tends to share certain features: conservative word choice, regular sentence structure, frequent use of phrasing learned in class. Those features overlap closely with what detection tools read as machine-generated — but what they actually indicate is a stage of language learning, not the origin of the text.

你可以拿什么自证

What you can show

写作过程本身构成证据，且无需额外操作即可留存。Google 文档与 Word 均自动 保存版本历史。版本历史、草稿、笔记，以及能够口头复述自身思路，这几项合并使用，其证明力 高于任何检测器分数。

The writing process is itself evidence, and it is retained without any extra step. Google Docs and Word both keep version history automatically. Version history, drafts, notes, and being able to talk through your own reasoning carry more probative weight together than any detector score.

The Council suggests using document editing history to help establish student-produced work and designing prompts that require engagement with course material and critical thinking.

Rice University Honor Council · univ/18-Rice.md

三十所学校里有二十所对「过程留证」未作表述，是沉默率最高的条款之一。本规范纳入该条款， 因为它是其中少数能够为守规者提供保护、而不只用于认定违规的规则。

Twenty of the thirty say nothing about keeping process evidence — one of the most silent provisions in the set. It is included here because it is one of the few rules that can work in a compliant student's favour rather than only serve to establish a violation.

申诉路径待补：由谁受理、多长时间内提出、需要提交什么材料、结果如何告知。 需校方确认后填入。

§6

国际部专章

For the international division

这一节是三十所大学都没有写、只能由我们自己写的部分。我们对全部三十份原文做了关键词扫描：

This is the part none of the thirty universities wrote, and only we can. We ran a keyword scan across all thirty source documents:

三十校语料 · 关键词命中 Keyword scan · thirty source files 0 校提到 admission · 0 校提到 personal statement · 0 校提到 college essay · 1 校提到 non-native（仅范德堡） 0 mention admission · 0 mention personal statement · 0 mention college essay · 1 mentions non-native (Vanderbilt only)

原因是这些政策的适用对象不同：大学政策的默认读者为已被录取的成年学生，且其英语水平 已通过入学要求的检验。这三项前提对国际部高中生均不成立。

The reason lies in who those policies address. A university policy assumes a reader who is an adult, already admitted, and whose English has already satisfied an entrance requirement. None of the three holds for students here.

6.1 当英语本身就是被考的能力

6.1 When English is the skill being assessed

在大学，AI 润色属于边缘问题：教师假定学生英语已达要求，所评估的是论证本身。国际部的情形 不同——相当一部分写作作业所评估的正是英语产出能力。因此同一操作在两种场景 下的性质并不相同。

At university, AI polishing is a marginal question: the instructor assumes English is already adequate and is assessing the argument. Here the situation differs — a substantial share of written work is assessed on the ability to produce English. The same operation therefore does not have the same character in the two settings.

因此本规范不以所用工具划分界限，而以所修改的对象划分。工具持续更替， 该判准不随之改变。

The line is therefore drawn not at which tool was used but at what was changed. Tools are replaced continually; this test is not affected by that.

你改的是举例档位 What you changedExampleLevel
错误 拼写、语法、时态、标点 AI-1 允许 Errors Spelling, grammar, tense, punctuation AI-1 permitted
表达 换词、改句式、让它「更地道」 AI-2 且须披露；若作业评的就是语言能力，禁止 Expression Different words, restructured sentences, making it "sound native" AI-2 with disclosure; prohibited where language itself is being assessed
内容 补论点、调结构、扩写、翻译整段 AI-2 起，须披露并附原稿 Content Added arguments, reordered structure, expansion, translating a whole passage AI-2 or above, with disclosure and your original draft

翻译需单独说明。先以中文完成、再由 AI 译为英文提交，所提交的文本未经过任何 一次英语层面的组织。本规范将其归入「改内容」：其性质不是润色，而是由他人完成表达，区别仅在 于所依据的原稿出自本人。

Translation needs a note of its own. Composing in Chinese and having AI render the result in English produces text that was never organised in English at any point. These guidelines classify that as changing content: in character it is not editing but expression carried out by another party, differing only in that the underlying draft is your own.

此处需要说明的是后果，而非评价。以 AI 修改后的文本呈现自身英语水平，在单次评分上可能获益； 但大学阶段的写作要求与此相同或更高，且课堂讨论、限时写作等场景不提供使用外部工具的条件， 也不接受事后声明。两者之间的差距会在那时显现。

What needs stating here is the consequence, not a judgment. Presenting AI-corrected text as your own level of English may raise a single grade. University writing demands are the same or higher, and settings such as seminar discussion and timed writing allow no external tool and accept no statement after the fact. The gap becomes visible at that point.

6.2 大学申请文书

6.2 College application essays

这一节的写法和全文不同。本校无权规定申请文书的 AI 规则——那些规则由申请平台 和每一所目标大学各自制定，而且每年在变。所以这里只做三件事：告诉你规则在哪、 本校的底线是什么、以及风险在哪里不对称。

This section works differently. This school does not get to set the AI rules for your applications — the platforms and each individual university set their own, and they change from year to year. So this section does only three things: tell you where the rules live, state this school's floor, and explain where the risk is asymmetric.

规则在哪里查Where the rules live

申请平台的条款与诚信声明 · 每一所目标大学的申请说明页。 必须当年现查。不要依赖学长学姐的经验，也不要依赖本规范的转述—— 本规范不对任何一所大学的当年规则作出保证。

The application platform's terms and integrity statement, and each target university's own admissions page. Check them in the year you apply. Do not rely on what an older student told you, and do not rely on this document — it makes no guarantee about any institution's current rules.

本校的底线This school's floor

一 · 不得提交非本人撰写的文书。

二 · 升学指导教师不为使用 AI 代写的文书背书，也不会在推荐信中为其作证。

1 · Do not submit an essay you did not write.

2 · Counselors will not endorse an AI-ghostwritten essay, and will not vouch for one in a recommendation.

两类认定的后果并不对称。课程作业被认定违规，影响范围为该门课程的成绩与 校内纪律记录；申请材料被认定违规，可能导致录取资格被撤销，入学后被发现亦可能导致学位被撤销。 此外，申请材料的审查不包含本规范 §5 所规定的对话与申诉环节——该程序由招生方 单方进行，申请人通常不会获得陈述机会。

The consequences of the two are not symmetrical. A finding on coursework affects that course's grade and an internal disciplinary record. A finding on an application can result in an offer being withdrawn, and if discovered after enrolment, in a degree being revoked. Further, an admissions review includes none of the conversation or appeal provided for in §5: the process is conducted by the admissions side alone, and the applicant is generally given no opportunity to respond.

Grey-zone cases

What rules can't settle, cases can. Each one gives the situation, the ruling, and the reason.

01 用 Grammarly 检查语法和拼写 Running Grammarly over your draft for grammar and spelling AI-1 允许 AI-1 · allowed

改的是错误，不产生新内容。仍须在声明里写明工具。

It fixes errors and produces no new content. Still name the tool in your statement.

02 先用中文写好，再让 AI 译成英文交上去 Writing it in Chinese, then having AI translate it into English 语言类作业违规 Violation in language work

属于「改内容」。交上去的是一篇你没有用英语想过一次的作文。 非语言类作业按 AI-2 处理，须披露并附中文原稿；语言能力测评中禁止。

This changes content. What you submit is an essay you never thought through in English. In non-language subjects treat it as AI-2 with disclosure and your Chinese original; in language assessments it is prohibited.

03 让 AI 列提纲，自己写全文 Having AI produce an outline, then writing the essay yourself AI-2 须披露 AI-2 · disclose

提纲是新内容，而且结构往往是作业要考的能力之一。写清楚提纲来自 AI、正文是你写的。

An outline is new content, and structure is often part of what the assignment assesses. State that the outline came from AI and the prose is yours.

04 让 AI 讲解一道看不懂的题，然后自己重做一遍 Asking AI to explain a problem you don't understand, then redoing it yourself AI-1 允许 AI-1 · allowed

这是把它当老师用，不是当枪手用。芝加哥大学的判准正好适用：AI 在做其他技术或人 本来就在做的事。前提是你真的重做了。

You are using it as a teacher, not a substitute. Chicago's test applies directly: it is doing what other technologies or people already do. The condition is that you actually redo it.

05 把同学的作业发给 AI，让它「参考着改我的」 Uploading a classmate's work so AI can "improve mine based on it" 违规 Violation

两重问题：这是 §4 的隐私违规（上传他人作品），而且和 AI 无关—— 换成你自己抄也一样违规。

Two problems at once: it breaches §4 by uploading someone else's work, and the AI is beside the point — copying it yourself would be equally a violation.

06 让 AI 找参考文献，直接引用没去核 Citing references AI supplied, without opening them 违规 Violation

§4 的核实责任。AI 编造看起来完全正常的文献是已知的高频故障， 而按本规范你对成品负 100% 的责任。合规做法：每条都打开原文看过再引。

This is the §4 verification duty. Fabricated but plausible-looking sources are a well-known failure mode, and you are 100% responsible for what you submit. Do this instead: open every source before you cite it.

07 让 AI 修改大学申请文书的语法 Having AI fix the grammar in a college application essay 本规范不作判定 Not ours to rule on

见 §6.2。这里的规则由申请平台和目标大学定，必须当年现查。 本校的底线只有一条：文书须由你本人撰写。

See §6.2. The rule belongs to the platform and the university, and must be checked in the year you apply. This school's floor is only that you wrote it.

08 编程作业里，用 AI 补全一段自己看得懂的代码 Letting AI complete a block of code you fully understand AI-2 须披露 AI-2 · disclose

产生了新内容，所以至少 AI-2，无论你看不看得懂。判断标准从来不是「我能不能理解」， 而是「这段是不是我写的」。另外：作业若考的就是写出这段代码的能力，教师可标为 AI-0。

It produces new content, so AI-2 at minimum, regardless of whether you understand it. The test is never "could I follow it" but "did I write it." Also: where the assignment assesses your ability to write that code, the teacher may mark it AI-0.

09 小组作业里，某个组员用了 AI 但没告诉其他人 A group member used AI and didn't tell the others 全组须在声明中写明 The group must disclose

小组提交的是一份作业，声明也只有一份。合规做法：动手之前先约定档位和分工， 提交时由全组共同确认声明内容。谁用的谁写清楚。

A group submits one piece of work and therefore one statement. Do this instead: agree the level and the split before you start, and confirm the statement together at submission. Whoever used it, names it.

10 用 AI 生成口语作业的讲稿，自己背下来讲 Generating a script for an oral assignment and memorising it 通常违规 Usually a violation

口语作业考的是你组织语言的能力，讲稿就是那个能力的产物。这类作业默认属于 「语言能力测评」，按 §6.1 归入 AI-0。

An oral task assesses your ability to organise language, and the script is exactly that output. These default to language assessment and fall under AI-0 per §6.1.

11 数学题自己算出答案，让 AI 补写解题步骤 Solving a maths problem yourself, then having AI write up the steps 通常违规 Usually a violation

数学作业要你写步骤，考的往往正是步骤本身——推理过程才是答案。 这属于「改内容」，且通常是被评估的那部分能力。

When a maths assignment asks for working, the working usually is the thing being assessed — the reasoning is the answer. This changes content, and typically the very content being graded.

12 实验报告里，让 AI 根据你的数据写讨论部分 Having AI write the discussion section from your own lab data AI-2，且须核实 AI-2 · verify carefully

数据是你的，解释不是。这里有个额外风险：AI 会写出看起来合理但你的数据并不支持 的结论，而按 §4 你对它负全责。合规做法：逐句对回你的原始数据。

The data is yours; the interpretation is not. There is an extra trap here: AI will write conclusions that read plausibly but your data does not support, and under §4 they are yours to answer for. Do this instead: check each claim back against your raw numbers.

这一节会继续长。最有价值的判例来自本校学生真实提出的问题，而不是我们坐在 办公室里能想出来的。遇到本节没覆盖的情况，把它提出来——它会变成第 13 条。
This section will keep growing. The most useful cases come from questions students actually ask, not from what we can invent at a desk. If you hit a situation this section doesn't cover, raise it — it becomes case 13.
