他们到底怎么规定的

What the rules actually say

本项目采集了三十所美国大学公开发布的 AI 使用政策，拆解为 12 项可比条款逐项对照， 并在此基础上起草了一份面向国际部高中生的使用规范。 两份文件都可以逐条追回它所依据的原文。

This project collected the published AI use policies of thirty U.S. universities, broke them into twelve comparable provisions, and drafted a set of guidelines for international-track high-school students on that basis. Every line in either document can be traced back to the text it rests on.

证据链

The chain of evidence

Traceability
01
规范的每条规则指向图谱Each rule points to the atlas

规范正文中的每一处数字断言，在构建时都会与数据集比对；对不上则构建中止。 目前 88 处断言、9 条引文全部通过。

Every numeric claim in the guidelines is checked against the dataset at build time, and a mismatch stops the build. Currently 88 assertions and 9 quotations pass.

02
图谱的每一格指向原文Each cell points to a source

三十份文件由三十个互相隔离的程序分别抽取，每个只被允许读它自己那一份， 并被禁止调用关于该校的既有知识；随后由对抗性审计重读同一份文件复核。

The thirty files were read by thirty isolated passes, each permitted to see only its own file and barred from drawing on prior knowledge of that institution, then re-checked by an adversarial audit against the same file.

03
每条原文经机器逐字比对Each quotation is matched verbatim

所有引文与其所属文件做精确字符串比对，对不上即降级并公开记录； 若某条引文出现在别校文件里，会被直接点名为串源。当前 234/234 通过。

Every quotation is matched exactly against the file it claims to come from; a miss is demoted and logged, and a quotation found in another school's file is named as cross-contamination. Currently 234/234 pass.

这条链的意义在于：本项目的结论都可以被推翻——只要有人回到原文，指出某一格判错了。 方法与已知问题在图谱的「方法与边界」一节中完整列出。

The point of the chain is that the conclusions here can be overturned: anyone can return to the source and show that a cell was coded wrongly. The method and its known limits are set out in full under "方法与边界" in the atlas.
