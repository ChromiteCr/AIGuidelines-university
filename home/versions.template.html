版本号的含义

Reading a version number

  • A 为项目代号；其后的数字是研究阶段，完成一个阶段加一，如 A4 → A5。
  • 阶段内完成一个可独立说明的功能或模块，加小写字母，从 a 起，如 A3 → A3a。
  • 修正、样式微调与文字修改，加末位数字，从 1 起，如 A3c → A3c1。
  • A is the project code; the number after it is the stage of the research, raised by one when a stage is complete, e.g. A4 → A5.
  • A feature or module completed within a stage adds a lower-case letter, from a, e.g. A3 → A3a.
  • A fix, style adjustment or wording change adds a final digit, from 1, e.g. A3c → A3c1.
