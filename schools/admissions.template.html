方法与局限

Method and limitations

  • 范围。以本科新生申请为主；研究生、转学和特定项目的规定单独标注，不外推到本科。
  • 证据。只有原文明确写出的许可与后果才记为证据。英文引文与采集时保存的来源逐字比对（仅标准化空白）；中文为整理者的分析，与原文分开标注。
  • 不作的推断。未检索到或未说明，不代表没有规定，更不代表允许。禁止 AI 代写不等于禁止一切辅助；允许语法检查也不等于允许改写或翻译。
  • 效力。正式规则、招生官建议与学生博客的效力不同，证据状态据此区分。
  • 共同来源。Common App 与 UC 系统单独列出；UC 的 3 所校区共用同一系统来源，不计为 3 份独立政策。
  • 时效。采集于 2026 年 9 月 24 日。Brown 与 Columbia 的本科页面当日无法访问，于 9 月 25 日补充检索取得正文。访问日不是生效日，提交申请前须按当季页面复核。
  • Scope. Chiefly first-year undergraduate applications. Rules for graduate, transfer and specific programmes are labelled as such and not extended to undergraduates.
  • Evidence. Only permissions and consequences the source states explicitly count as evidence. English quotations were matched verbatim against the sources saved at collection (whitespace normalised only); the Chinese is the collector's analysis and is marked apart from the source text.
  • Inferences not drawn. Nothing found, or nothing said, does not mean there is no rule, still less that a use is allowed. A ban on AI ghost-writing is not a ban on all help; permission to check grammar is not permission to rewrite or translate.
  • Force. Formal rules, admissions officers' advice and student blogs carry different weight; the evidence status separates them.
  • Shared sources. Common App and the UC system are listed on their own; the 3 UC campuses share one system source and are not counted as 3 independent policies.
  • Currency. Collected on 24 September 2026. The undergraduate pages of Brown and Columbia could not be reached that day and were retrieved on 25 September. An access date is not an effective date; check the pages for the current cycle before applying.
