## AI Use and Recommendation Guide

- Publishing unit: UCLA Digital & Technology Solutions (DTS)
- Official source: https://dts.ucla.edu/initiatives/ai/ai-use-recommendation-guide

---

UCLA supports “the responsible, transparent, ethical, and risk-informing use of generative AI tools” in education, research, and administration.

### Data protection

Most approved tools are restricted to low-risk data, and users must check each tool’s permitted data classification. Unless explicitly approved in a secure environment, users may not enter:

- FERPA-protected student information, including IDs, grades, and coursework;
- health or personnel information;
- “Unpublished research, IP, or grant proposals”;
- export-controlled or restricted data; or
- malicious or harmful prompts.

### Academic integrity

“Students may not present AI-generated work as their own without acknowledgement. Misuse constitutes plagiarism under the UCLA Student Conduct Code.”

## Reflective and responsible use in teaching and learning

- Publishing unit: UCLA Teaching and Learning Center
- Additional official source: https://teaching.ucla.edu/resources/teaching-guides/using-generative-ai-reflectively-and-responsibly-in-teaching-and-learning

The resource advises instructors to state their GenAI policy explicitly. A no-use model treats confirmed use of AI writing tools as academic dishonesty. A limited-use model requires AI-generated content to be reviewed, edited, and verified for accuracy before submission.

When students present a significant amount of minimally edited AI-generated text, the guidance says they should identify the specific tool and follow appropriate citation guidance.

## High-school adaptation takeaways

- Distinguish approved school AI platforms from consumer accounts.
- Students must acknowledge AI-generated material and cannot present it as their own.
- Require review, editing, fact-checking, and citation when AI use is permitted.
- Keep FERPA-like student records, health information, unpublished research, and intellectual property out of unapproved systems.
